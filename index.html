HOW TO PLAY

Relay Command — farm first, fight later

You wake up in a quiet system: buy Relay Drones, link Worlds — moons, asteroid belts, planets — farm gold like a cozy idle game. The mycelium sleeps at the rim… until your first weapon wakes it. Zoom out. Then survive it by chaining Station → World lanes and hunting its hearts.

Scouts & fog of war — chart the dark

You wake knowing only home waters around the Station. The rest is unknown — worlds, Bloom and all hidden until scanned. Buy a Scout Drone and CLICK unknown dark space: it flies out, runs a radar sweep, and permanently reveals a circular area. Scans reveal what was already there — including the Mycelium, which was growing in the dark the whole time. Charting a world pays 8g, derelicts hide +25g salvage.

Building & caps

Click a weapon in the bottom bar, then click the map to place it in orbit around the Station or a linked world (right-click cancels). Press B or the – button to collapse the bar and see behind it. Caps, always visible above the buttons: Station 3 weapons + 3 Hangar bays, each world 2 weapons. When a ring is full, upgrade instead of building.

Station tech — grow your base

CLICK the Station itself to open its tech panel. Solar Array mints gold on a timer (more wings appear per level); Research Lab boosts ALL pierce damage (watch the dishes sprout). Five levels each, costs rising — your base visibly grows more advanced all run long. Command Center speeds all drones and scouts (+8%/lv); Navigation Array extends uplink reach (+35/lv).

Drones & trade lanes — in order

1. Buy a Relay Drone in the bottom bar (available immediately — this is your idle game). 2. CLICK a glowing gold world — worlds ARE planets and moons here, so click the planet itself — to send the idle drone there: it opens the lane, mines ore, and ferries gold home per trip (5–8g by world). The drone keeps chasing its world as it orbits, and the lane stays glued to the planet. Watch the lane: bright packets are cargo heading home, dim ones are empty drones heading out. Frontier worlds start UNSURVEYED (?) — buy a Scout Drone and click dark space to scan whole regions, or click a ? world to chart it directly: charting pays 8g, derelicts hide +25g salvage, and strange discoveries happen. 3. Build a Hangar Bay first: +1 drone slot, +130 uplink range, and faster crews per level. At Lv3 choose THROUGHPUT (faster mining and better delivery) or RANGE (longer relays). 4. Click its world again to recall it. Purple-smothered worlds refuse drones until purged. Crews fly through the cloud, not around it — dense purple clings (slow) and burns (hull damage), so park a weapon on threatened worlds to escort their crews.

Routes pay differently (strategy)

Moons are safe and fast (5g, quick crews), Belts are the industrial core (7g), Planets pay frontier rates (8g, slow), Derelicts leak tech (+2 purge per delivery). Infected ground pays +2g hazard pay — hold it or purge it. A gun parked on a world escorts its crews (faster mining).

Reaching far worlds (heart hunting)

Four ways outward: Station range (~150), Hangar uplink (+120 per Hangar level — the long game), parent chains (link the world nearer the Station first), and relay hops (any LINKED world within 300px bounces drones further). Hover a world for its exact status; the log tells you what a locked one needs. Late game = hop lane-by-lane toward a ♥, park Railguns in range, burn it down.

Build keys are assigned left-to-right to the options currently available. Pulse Laser starts at 1; new weapons, drones, and modules receive the next number as they unlock.

The voxel cloud

The mycelium is a living pixel cloud creeping in from the rim of a big scrollable galaxy (drag to pan, wheel to zoom, WASD/arrows pan too — there is no edge). It smothers worlds, blocks build spots, and drains the Station on touch. If voxels reach a world it slides EXPOSED → INFECTED (slow mining) → CORRUPTED (drones refused) → OVERRUN (feeds the cloud) — shoot the purple off to cure it. Every tower shoots automatically — Plasma splashes crowds, Cryo freezes spread, Tesla arcs, Railgun (once the Bloom stirs) snipes from extreme range.

Hearts & pods — the Bloom never ends

♥ Hearts pulse at the rim and fountain new cloud. Burn them down for breathing room (+40g, +25 purge each) — but the organism returns: quiet, then tendrils, then new clusters, then new hearts. Hearts that hold together long enough merge into a ◉ Bloom Core: a siege organ inside a regenerating colony that commands the ground around it — new hearts will not root in a core's shadow. Its dark crown roots are real tendrils: shoot their pale tips to stall and sever them like any growth. While a core lives, the whole Bloom quickens — faster creep, faster tendrils, more pods. Clear the purple around it first (the colony shields it), then break the core — its territory stays behind for you to reclaim. Shoot a growing tendril's pale tip to stall it — break it and the tendril severs. Spore pods bloom on the frontier and seed fast: pop them (+12g, +5 purge). Purging voxels still pays and hits gold milestones at 25/50/75%. Every heart grows within Railgun reach of holdable ground — if it looks far, push your lanes forward and park the big gun on a linked world.

The Bloom keeps growing

Buy Relay Drones to extend the lane chain and bring ore home. The living cloud creeps faster the longer you hold; every so often a biological surge lunges inward.

Upgrades — Branching!

Click a tower → the panel shows its role, its slot (e.g. Station 2/3), and a live preview of the next level. Weapons specialize at Lv2 → Lv3: Power improves each weapon's signature output, while Pierce favors penetration and precision. Hangars choose logistics instead: THROUGHPUT improves mining and delivery, RANGE extends relays. Sell refunds 60%.

Core Purge — the standing emergency

The circular CORE PURGE dial beside the build bar is online from the start (Space, 200g, long cooldown) — it is the station's emergency system, not a reward. The station charges, shudders, then discharges a huge pulse: nearby voxels snap, young growth burns, hearts reel. It buys time, never victory — far tendrils keep growing. Expensive on purpose: reach for it when the station itself is threatened, not as an opener.

Strike Wing — the other arm

Later still, the STRIKE WING dial (opposite side, X, 260g, long refit) launches 4 disposable attack craft at a point you choose. Flying in, every craft fires piercing slugs down the attack lane — use it to pierce into ground your towers cannot reach. Over the target they strafe and carve — but each has 1 HP, and the cloud eats ships. Send them to break open a smothered world or a threatened lane, then hold the ground with real defenses.

Forward Outposts — the civilization phase

Max out the station (Solar 4, Lab 3, Command 3, Nav 2, Strike Wing online, 5 lanes, Hangar 3) and click it: FORWARD OUTPOST (600g, max 2, ever). Survey open ground — the ghost shows relay reach and Bloom presence — and a construction fleet will fly out and build it live. The moment construction completes, the outpost automatically opens a lifeline trade lane to the nearest resource world (SUPPLY LINK: ACTIVE) — no drone slot needed, reserved for the outpost alone. An outpost relays nearby worlds into your network, takes short deliveries, repairs crews, and holds 2 weapons. No purge, no wing, no tech — and the Bloom will come for it, with warnings before it falls. Choose like it matters.

Voxels, not walkers

There are no creep units anymore — the cloud is the enemy. Aim is automatic: towers pick the most station-threatening voxel in range. Keep lanes clear and never let purple touch the Station.

Hotkeys: number keys build, Space CORE PURGE (200g emergency pulse), X STRIKE WING (260g fleet), B collapse the build bar, WASD/arrows pan, wheel zoom, Esc cancel/deselect, P pause.

Survive long enough to unlock the WARP MAP — Fertile, Infested, or Ancient systems, each with its own bounty and hunger. Hearts keep returning (the PRIMARY is fortified, the last one screams) — there is no final clearing, only the expedition. You can warp on, keep purging, or stand down. Far worlds are RICH: the deeper you expand, the more ore each trip brings home.
